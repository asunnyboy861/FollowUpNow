## Description
FollowUpNow is the smart follow-up reminder app that helps you never lose a client again. Whether you are a sales professional, entrepreneur, or business owner, FollowUpNow ensures every follow-up happens on time with the right message.

SMART REMINDERS WITH QUICK ACTIONS
Get timely notifications with one-tap actions. Call, text, email, snooze, or mark done right from your lock screen. No more opening the app to take action on a reminder.

AI-POWERED FOLLOW-UP MESSAGES
Let AI draft professional, personalized follow-up messages for you. Based on your client history and last interaction, FollowUpNow generates natural-sounding content ready to send in seconds. On-device AI keeps your data private.

VISUAL PIPELINE TRACKING
See where every client stands with a clean Kanban pipeline view. Track contacts from first touch to conversion. Drag and drop to update stages instantly.

REUSABLE TEMPLATES
Save time with built-in follow-up templates for common scenarios like post-meeting follow-ups, proposal check-ins, and monthly touch bases. Create your own templates for your unique workflow.

CONTACT IMPORT
Import clients directly from your iOS Contacts. Get started in seconds, not hours. No manual data entry needed.

ICLOUD SYNC
Keep your follow-ups in sync across all your devices. Your data travels with you wherever you go.
