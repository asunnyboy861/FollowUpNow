Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Download FollowUpNow from the App Store and follow the onboarding tutorial. You can import your existing contacts from iOS Contacts to get started quickly.

How do I create a follow-up reminder?

Tap the + button on the Home screen, select a client, set a due date and channel (call, text, or email), and save. You'll receive a notification when it's time to follow up.

How do I use AI to generate follow-up messages?

When creating a follow-up, tap "Generate Follow-Up Message" in the AI Assistant section. The AI will draft a personalized message based on the client's history and your last interaction. This feature requires a Pro or Pro+ subscription.

How do I import contacts?

Go to Settings > Data > Import from Contacts. FollowUpNow will request access to your iOS Contacts and import them as clients.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Data > Export Data (CSV). This will generate a CSV file that you can share via email, AirDrop, or save to Files.

Subscription FAQ

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone/iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Find FollowUpNow and tap it
  5. Choose "Cancel Subscription" or change your plan

Or visit: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open FollowUpNow, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Pro or Pro+ access will be restored.

What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro ($14.99 one-time): Unlimited clients, on-device AI follow-ups, unlimited templates, notification actions, data export.

Pro+ ($3.99/month or $29.99/year): All Pro features plus cloud AI generation, iCloud sync, smart suggestions, and priority support.

What happens if I cancel my Pro+ subscription?

You can continue using Pro+ features until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll revert to the free version with limited clients and follow-ups. Your data is always preserved.

I purchased Pro but it's not showing

Go to Settings > "Restore Purchases" to restore your Pro access. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
