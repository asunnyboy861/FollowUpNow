Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Introduction

FollowUpNow ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Client Data: Names, companies, email addresses, phone numbers, and notes you enter about your clients.
  • Follow-Up Data: Follow-up titles, notes, due dates, and channels you create.
  • Interaction Records: Summaries of interactions you log with clients.
  • Template Content: Custom follow-up templates you create.
  • Support Messages: If you contact us through the in-app support form, we collect your email address and message content.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Notification Preferences: Your notification settings and reminder preferences.
  • App Usage Data: Basic analytics about feature usage to improve the app experience.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not collect browsing history.
  • We do not collect financial information.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the FollowUpNow service.
  • To send follow-up reminders and notifications you've requested.
  • To generate AI-powered follow-up content (on-device or via cloud API).
  • To sync your data across devices via iCloud (Pro+ subscribers only).
  • To respond to support inquiries.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

Data Storage

Local Storage

Your primary data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data remains on your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

iCloud Sync (Pro+ Only)

If you subscribe to Pro+ and enable iCloud sync, your data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. We cannot access your iCloud data. Apple's privacy policies apply to iCloud storage.

AI Content Generation

Pro users: AI content is generated on-device using Apple Foundation Models. No data is sent to external servers.

Pro+ users: If on-device generation is unavailable, content may be generated via a cloud API. Only the prompt text (client name, interaction summary) is sent. Generated content is not stored on our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Apple: For App Store distribution, in-app purchases, and iCloud sync (as governed by Apple's Privacy Policy).
  • As Required by Law: If compelled by legal process or regulatory requirement.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time by deleting individual clients, follow-ups, or by uninstalling the app. Upon uninstallation, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time within the app.
  • Export your data in CSV format via Settings > Data > Export Data.
  • Disable iCloud sync at any time via Settings.
  • Opt out of notifications via iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

FollowUpNow is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. On-device data is protected by iOS encryption. iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
